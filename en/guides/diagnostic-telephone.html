What is phone diagnostics for?

Diagnostics identifies Android settings that may interfere with calls and voicemail notifications. It checks the default Phone app, notification permissions and priority, full-screen display, voicemail signals and some manufacturer settings.

A successful check does not prove that a real call or voicemail alert will work. Always test both.

Open the diagnostics screen

  1. Open Family mode and enter your PIN.
  2. Choose Calls in ClikoSpido.
  3. Tap the diagnostic button in the phone-function check section.
  4. Follow any settings shortcuts and refresh the results afterward.

Understand the results

An OK result means the detected setting meets the expected condition. A correction is needed if a setting or permission requires action. A check is needed when ClikoSpido cannot verify the setting automatically. Android setting names vary by device.

Incoming call checks

Check that ClikoSpido is the default Phone app, notifications are enabled, incoming-call notification priority is high enough, and full-screen call display is allowed where applicable. Android 14 and later may require a special full-screen permission. Make a test call with the screen locked.

Voicemail checks

The screen checks voicemail detection permission, alert settings, monitoring and observed signals. The voicemail notification test only creates a local test alert; it does not create a real voicemail.

  1. Enable notifications and voicemail detection if needed.
  2. Run the notification test and check the notification panel.
  3. Leave a real voicemail from another phone.
  4. Refresh diagnostics to see whether Android or the network reported it.

Visual voicemail and mobile carriers

Some phones use visual voicemail managed by a carrier or the original Phone app. ClikoSpido does not currently support visual voicemail. Detecting a possible handler does not establish that the service is enabled or blocking alerts.

If real alerts still fail, contact the carrier about standard voicemail or restore the original Phone app. Understand the consequences before changing carrier settings.

Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO

On some MIUI/HyperOS phones, lock-screen display, background windows, auto-start and battery restrictions can interfere with calls. The manufacturer settings may not be verifiable automatically. Open the suggested Android settings, allow lock-screen display where available, then test a call.

Ask for assistance

Record the phone manufacturer, model, Android version, symptoms and relevant diagnostic results. Do not share private numbers, contact names or screenshots containing personal data.
