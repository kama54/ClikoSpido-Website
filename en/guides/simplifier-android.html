Make an Android phone simpler

You do not always need a new device to make Android easier. Begin with the phone’s existing display settings, then consider a simpler home launcher if needed.

Identify daily tasks

Start with a short list: calls, messages, photos and contacting family. Make those actions easy to find.

Improve readability

Open Android Settings and look for Display, Font size or Display size. Names differ by phone brand.

Remove distractions

Keep only useful shortcuts on the first screen and reduce nonessential notifications.

Test together

Let the person find a contact and place a call. Adjust based on what they actually find easy.

ClikoSpido can provide a different, larger-button home screen on a compatible Android smartphone. It does not replace every app.
