Getting started with ClikoSpido

Install the app, create a Family PIN, and choose the home app and default Phone app separately.

Install from Google Play

ClikoSpido offers a simplified home screen for compatible Android phones. Installing it does not delete your contacts, photos or messages.

  1. Open the ClikoSpido listing on Google Play.
  2. Tap Install, then Open.
  3. Check the selected language before creating your PIN.

Google Play →

First launch and Family PIN

The welcome screen lets you choose one of ten languages and protect Family mode settings with a PIN.

  1. Choose your language.
  2. Under Create Family PIN, enter 4–8 digits.
  3. Enter the same PIN again and tap Start.
  4. Keep the PIN somewhere safe. You will need it to change protected settings.

Why choose ClikoSpido as your home screen?

When ClikoSpido is the default home app, the Android Home button opens its simplified interface rather than your usual launcher.

  1. Open Family mode and enter the PIN.
  2. Choose Phone home screen.
  3. Tap Set ClikoSpido as home.
  4. Select ClikoSpido in Android and confirm. Use Refresh status if necessary.

You can switch back to Android Home whenever you like. ClikoSpido remains installed and retains its settings.

Why make ClikoSpido the default Phone app?

This is optional and separate from the home app. It enables ClikoSpido's large answer, reject, hang-up, speaker and keypad controls, plus access to Recents when the necessary permissions are granted.

  1. Go to Family mode → Calls in ClikoSpido.
  2. Read and acknowledge the confirmation, then tap Show calls in ClikoSpido.
  3. Choose ClikoSpido as the default Phone app in Android and grant necessary permissions.
  4. Test incoming and outgoing calls, including on the lock screen.

Without this role, the original Phone app handles incoming calls. Voicemail alerts depend on the carrier and device. If problems persist, choose Switch back to Android Phone.

Check everything after setup

Make sure Home opens ClikoSpido, contacts and photos are correct, incoming calls appear when locked, outgoing calls and speaker work, and a real test voicemail triggers an alert. Make sure the helper knows the Family PIN and how to restore the original Phone app. Use Phone diagnostics to check settings.
