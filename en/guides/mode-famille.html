Open Family mode

Open Family settings on the ClikoSpido home screen and enter the 4–8 digit PIN created at first launch. These protected settings are for the user or a trusted helper.

Premium

View and manage ClikoSpido's paid options.

  1. Review the available monthly subscription and lifetime purchase options. Prices are shown by Google Play.
  2. Use Restore purchases if you already bought Premium with the relevant Google account.
  3. Use Manage subscription in Google Play to cancel a subscription. Purchases only work once the Play products are configured.

Phone home screen

Choose ClikoSpido as the phone's main home screen.

  1. Tap “Set ClikoSpido as home” and select it in Android's home app settings. Some options require Premium.
  2. The Home button will then open ClikoSpido. Use “Refresh status” to check the result.
  3. To restore your previous home screen, choose the option to return to Android Home and select your old launcher. ClikoSpido settings are kept.

Calls in ClikoSpido

Choose whether calls use ClikoSpido's interface or the usual Android Phone app.

  1. To show incoming calls with large ClikoSpido buttons, acknowledge the warning and select the option to display calls in ClikoSpido. Android will ask you to set the default Phone app.
  2. Test incoming and outgoing calls, the ringtone, lock screen, Bluetooth and dual SIM if used.
  3. If anything fails, restore the original Android Phone app. Some devices also require full-screen call permission.

Emergency call settings

Select a loved one and prepare an emergency text message.

  1. Select a person in My loved ones or enter a name and valid phone number.
  2. Edit the emergency SMS text and tap Save settings.
  3. The SMS is only prepared in your regular messaging app: the user still needs to tap Send. This contacts a loved one and does not replace calling emergency services.

Customize home

Choose which large shortcuts appear on the home screen and in what order.

  1. Use the switches next to each shortcut to show or hide it.
  2. Use the up and down arrows to rearrange the shortcuts.
  3. Open Senior mode preview to check the layout before returning to settings. Customization may require Premium.

My loved ones

Add loved ones to the home screen for quick contact.

  1. Import a contact from the phone or type their name and number manually.
  2. To set a photo, choose Add photo to profile; the Android Contacts editor opens.
  3. On the home screen, tap a loved one's photo to call them. SMS opens the phone's usual messaging app.

Display

Adjust text size, home layout, loved ones' photos and appearance.

  1. Choose Normal, Large or Very large text and test readability on the user's device.
  2. Where available, choose one or two home-screen columns and one, two or three loved ones per row. Fewer per row means larger photos.
  3. Enable dark mode or high contrast if it helps. Some layouts require Premium.

Language

Choose the language of ClikoSpido's menus and buttons.

  1. Tap one of the ten available languages.
  2. The change applies immediately to ClikoSpido but does not change Android's system language.
  3. If you pick the wrong language, return to this Family mode page to choose another.

Allowed apps

Choose which other apps appear in ClikoSpido.

  1. In Family mode, tick the apps you want to make accessible.
  2. Untick an app to remove its shortcut from ClikoSpido's Apps page.
  3. This does not uninstall the app or block it elsewhere in Android. Custom selection may require Premium.

Change PIN

Change the PIN that protects Family mode.

  1. Enter your current PIN first.
  2. Choose a new 4-to-8-digit PIN and enter it again to confirm.
  3. Tap Save new PIN and store it safely. This help cannot view or recover your PIN.
