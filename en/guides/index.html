Why try ClikoSpido?

ClikoSpido Launcher provides a clearer alternative home screen on compatible Android phones, without ads.

  • Photo contacts help users recognize and call loved ones.
  • Family Mode lets relatives choose permitted apps, customize the display and protect selected settings with a PIN.
