Set up a parent’s smartphone

Setting up a parent’s phone works best when they make the important choices with you. Aim for independence, not unnecessary restrictions.

Begin with their habits

Ask which contacts and features they use each day.

Bring loved ones forward

Add a small set of verified contacts with photos for quick calls.

Check the messaging app

Make sure the Messages shortcut opens the app they know. Practice sending and receiving.

Agree on emergency contacts

Check the saved number and explain that a shortcut cannot guarantee emergency assistance.
