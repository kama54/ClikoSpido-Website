Calls and lock screen

I have to unlock my phone before answering a call.

Open Family mode → Calls in ClikoSpido → phone diagnostics. Verify the default Phone role, notifications and full-screen display permission. On Xiaomi/Redmi also check lock-screen, background and battery settings. Test with the phone locked after each change.

Phone diagnostics →

My screen stays black when someone calls.

Check incoming-call settings first. If the problem appears during a conversation, make sure a case or screen protector is not covering the proximity sensor. Test in standby and off the charger. If calls are unreliable, temporarily restore the original Phone app.

Why are the large Answer and Reject buttons missing?

Setting the home app is not enough. Open Family mode → Calls in ClikoSpido and select ClikoSpido as the default Phone app. Check notification permissions.

Why can't I see recent calls?

The built-in call history requires ClikoSpido to be the default Phone app with the necessary permissions. Otherwise Recents may open the original Phone app.

I cannot hear the ringtone.

Check ringtone volume and Do Not Disturb, then notification priority, permissions and battery restrictions. Make a test call.

How do I restore the original Phone app?

Go to Family mode → Calls in ClikoSpido → Switch back to Android Phone. Select your original Phone app in Android. ClikoSpido can remain your home app.

Speaker or keypad controls do not work during a call.

Check the default Phone role, update ClikoSpido on Google Play and reproduce the problem in a test call. If an essential function fails, restore the original Phone app and contact support.

Voicemail and notifications

I receive no voicemail notifications.

Check notifications and voicemail detection permission in diagnostics. Run the notification test and leave a real voicemail. If only the test works, the carrier or phone may not supply a compatible signal, or visual voicemail may be involved.

Phone diagnostics →

Diagnostics reports visual voicemail. Is this a problem?

Not necessarily. A detected handler does not prove the service is active or blocking notifications. ClikoSpido does not support visual voicemail; if alerts fail, contact your carrier or restore the original Phone app.

Why does the notification test work but real voicemail does not?

The test produces only a local alert. Real notifications require a usable indicator from the network or Android; a successful test cannot guarantee real alerts.

Where can I listen to my messages?

Open Call → Recents. You can also hold down the 1 key on the keypad when the phone or carrier has set up that shortcut.

Why do alerts vary between carriers?

Voicemail signals depend on the SIM, network, device and type of voicemail. Even two phones running the same app can behave differently. Diagnostics helps distinguish settings problems from missing carrier signals.

I still get no voicemail alerts after all checks.

Temporarily choose the original Android Phone app as default. You can keep ClikoSpido as your simplified home screen.

Home, display and contacts

ClikoSpido does not open when I press Home.

Open Family mode → Phone home screen and set ClikoSpido as home. Confirm in Android and refresh the status.

How do I restore my old home screen?

In Family mode → Phone home screen, choose to return to Android Home and select your original launcher.

Text or photos are too small.

In Family mode → Display, select larger text or fewer people per row, depending on available options. Some layouts require Premium.

A loved one's photo is missing or wrong.

Open Family mode → My loved ones and check the contact. Choose Add photo to profile, save the photo in Android Contacts and return to ClikoSpido.

Why does Messages open Google Messages or Samsung Messages?

This is normal. ClikoSpido uses your phone's default SMS/RCS app and does not manage your conversations.

An app is missing from ClikoSpido.

Choose shortcuts in Family mode → Allowed apps. This does not install or uninstall Android apps. Full customization may require Premium.

Emergency, security and purchases

Does the emergency SMS send automatically?

No. ClikoSpido prepares the recipient and message in your messaging app; you must verify and tap Send. It contacts a loved one, not emergency services.

I forgot my Family PIN.

The guide cannot display or recover the PIN. Check your personal notes. Avoid uninstalling without checking whether settings will be lost, and contact support for version-specific options.

How do I restore Premium?

Go to Family mode → Premium → Restore purchases using the Google account that made the purchase. Google Play manages purchase status and pricing.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open Google Play subscription settings for the purchasing account and manage the ClikoSpido subscription. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

How do I get device-specific help?

Email contact@gogostudio.app with the make, model, Android version, symptoms and relevant diagnostic results. Do not include private numbers or contact data.
